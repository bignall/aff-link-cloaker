# v0.3.1: JVZoo product names fixed

Fixes the browser extension naming most JVZoo products "Seller Information".

## Update

Download both zips below.

- **Plugin:** upload **aff-link-cloaker-0.3.1.zip** under **Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin** and choose **Replace current with uploaded**.
- **Extension:** unzip **aff-link-cloaker-extension-0.1.1.zip** over your existing extension folder, then click the reload icon on the extension's card in `chrome://extensions`.

## Fixes

- **Real product names:** the extension now reads each product's name and your affiliate link from JVZoo's own "Get Link" boxes on the Approved Products page. Version 0.1.0 mistook the product's "Seller Information", "Integrations" and "Bonuses" links for its name.
- **No stray products:** JVZoo's "Product of the day" banner, and other links that use JVZoo's own affiliate ID instead of yours, are no longer picked up.
- **Repairing links you already imported:** scan your Approved Products page again and click **Create cloaked links**. Links named "Seller Information" (or "Integrations" or "Bonuses") get the real product name, and their address is updated to match, e.g. `/go/productspilot-megabundle/`. Links you've named yourself aren't touched.

If 0.1.0 created a link for a product that isn't on your list, it came from the "Product of the day" banner. Its affiliate link contains `/c/1/`, which isn't your affiliate ID, so delete it.
